01 / The problem

Your site works against the business, not for it.

Right when a client or a lender looks you up, it reads like 2013 instead of 47 years of expertise.

The legal pages were written for a different company. The Terms of Use name "Creditline Australia Australia Ltd", which does not exist, and carry College Capital's company number, so every protective clause protects a different business.

The Privacy Policy is 88 percent identical to College Capital's, including their typos, and twelve years out of date. The complaints page gets your customer's core right backwards.

"Mount Waverly" and postcode 3124 sit on every page. The suburb is Mount Waverley, 3149, and the address has been stale since the March 2024 move.

On a phone there is no menu, and the only enquiry form is hidden. From the device most clients use, there is no way to reach you.

Underneath it all: a 2013 template on software that reached end of life in 2022, with no security headers, four versions of the company's name, and a footer with no company name, no ACN, and no AFCA membership number.

56 verified defects. Every one found in the delivered code.

The full audit accompanies this deck
02 / The solution

A dark, editorial site reimagined around language that was already yours.

Peculiar commercial loans. The idea was sitting in your existing site, hidden. The new site opens on it: Others see risk. We see the unicorn.

Your colours kept, not replaced. The blue deepened to imperial, the lime you already carry sharpened. The same scheme, modernised, never a rebrand.

Every borrowed and broken page rebuilt clean from the ground up, and every fact verified before it was typed: one company name, one licence number, the right suburb, the right postcode.

The commercial side built in: enquiry on every screen, finance calculators assessed the way a lender assesses, and your real Google reviews doing the talking.

Built by hand. No template, no WordPress, no plugins. Nothing to hack and nothing to expire.
